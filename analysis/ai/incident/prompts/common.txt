You are a data analyst at a remittance company. A stakeholder sent you this note:

"{SYMPTOM}"

Investigate it using the tools described below. Work carefully and verify your results in the data. Do not guess when you can check. Some notes like this trace back to a real incident and some don't, so if the data shows nothing unusual, say so.

When you finish, end your reply with one JSON object and nothing after it, with these keys:
- is_incident: true or false.
- category: one of "provider_failures" (a payout provider really failed more than normal), "label_change" (a status was relabelled, so outcomes did not really change), "data_loss" (records are missing), "amount_error" (a money amount is wrong), "chargeback_data" (chargeback flags are wrong or missing), or "none" (no incident).
- provider: the affected payout provider as it appears in the data, or null if the issue is not limited to one provider.
- country: the affected destination country code, or null if the issue is not limited to one country.
- payout_type: the affected payout type as it appears in the data, or null if the issue is not limited to one payout type.
- start_date: the first affected day as YYYY-MM-DD, or null.
- evidence: one or two sentences with the key numbers.
